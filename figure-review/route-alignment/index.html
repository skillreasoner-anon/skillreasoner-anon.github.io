What These Illustrations Mean

The route prefix comes from a recorded V6.0 rollout. Its final approach and the rotated goal poses are constructed illustrations, not an executed alignment sequence. All displayed scores are computed with the paper’s route-progress metric and polygon-overlap calculation; no score is inferred from distance alone.

Route colors show the score at each pose, including any temporary decreases. The illustrated endpoint is 66%, not 100%. The full color scale stays fixed across variants. The score bars in B show weighted contributions: route progress supplies 50 percentage points; overlap supplies the remainder.

These figures explain the metric, not a claim about how long alignment takes. They do not establish that alignment is harder from this example alone.
